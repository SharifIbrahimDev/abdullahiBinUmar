# Privacy Policy for Sheikh Abdullahi Bin Umar Audio App

**Effective Date:** September 9, 2026  
**Last Updated:** September 9, 2026  

This Privacy Policy explains how the **Sheikh Abdullahi Bin Umar** mobile application ("the App", "we", "us", or "our"), developed and maintained by **K99Consult / Sharif Ibrahim**, handles user information.

---

### 1. Information We Collect
The **Sheikh Abdullahi Bin Umar** app is designed to provide seamless access to Islamic audio lectures, tafseer, and sermons without compromising user privacy.

* **No Personal Data Collected:** We do **not** collect, store, or transmit any personally identifiable information (such as your name, email address, phone number, physical address, or precise location).
* **No Account Creation Required:** You can access all lectures, features, and audio controls without registering or creating an account.
* **No Third-Party Analytics / Tracking:** We do not use third-party behavioral analytics or advertising trackers.

---

### 2. Device Storage & Offline Downloads
* **Local Storage:** The app allows you to download audio lectures directly to your device storage for offline playback. These files are stored locally on your device and are never uploaded to any external server.
* **User Preferences:** Local preferences (such as playback speed, theme settings, language preferences, and bookmarked lectures) are stored strictly locally on your device using Android Shared Preferences.

---

### 3. Permissions Used
The app requests minimal permissions strictly necessary for core functionality:
* `INTERNET` & `ACCESS_NETWORK_STATE`: Required to stream audio lectures and check connectivity.
* `FOREGROUND_SERVICE` & `FOREGROUND_SERVICE_MEDIA_PLAYBACK`: Required to provide uninterrupted audio playback with lock screen / notification media controls when the app is minimized.
* `WAKE_LOCK`: Ensures continuous audio playback without interruption when the screen turns off.
* `POST_NOTIFICATIONS`: Allows the app to display playback controls in the system notification bar on Android 13+.

---

### 4. Children’s Privacy
The app does not collect personal data from anyone, including children under the age of 13. All content is family-friendly, educational, and religious.

---

### 5. Third-Party Services & External Links
The app may contain links to external Islamic resources or contact links (such as WhatsApp, Telegram, or email). When you click on third-party links, you are directed to their respective platforms which operate under their own privacy policies.

---

### 6. Security of Your Information
Because the app does not collect or transmit personal information over the internet, your personal data remains completely private and secure on your own device.

---

### 7. Changes to This Privacy Policy
We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any updates will be reflected on this page with a revised "Last Updated" date.
